About BTS

A modern name.
Established electrical expertise.

BTS is the trading identity of Baltic Electric Ltd. We combine hands-on electrical knowledge with a professional approach to safety, technical compliance and long-term client relationships.

Our Managing Director began his career as an SSE apprentice and has 10 years' experience in the electrical industry, covering domestic and commercial work, inspection, testing and certification.
